Starting this blog mainly to document things I'd otherwise forget -- vulnerability research, CTF solutions, tooling I've built, and the occasional deep dive into something that caught my attention.

## What I'll post about

- **Vulnerability research** -- interesting bugs, attack vectors, exploitation techniques
- **CTF writeups** -- mostly TryHackMe and HackTheBox
- **Security tooling** -- automation scripts and tools I build for work and side projects
- **Threat modeling** -- how I approach mapping attack surfaces

No promises on a regular posting schedule.

## About me

Application Security Engineer at Zoho. I do security assessments on web, cloud, and iOS apps -- 110+ and counting. I also build security tools, design CTF challenges for internal training, and spend probably too much time on HackerOne and Bugcrowd.
